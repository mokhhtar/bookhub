James Oliver Rigney Jr. was an American author of epic fantasy who wrote under the pen name Robert Jordan. He is best known for his Wheel of Time series, which comprises 14 books and a prequel novel. 

With 90 million copies sold worldwide, this work ranks among the highest-selling book series of all time.
