Emily St. John Mandel is a Canadian novelist and essayist who has written six novels. Her acclaimed works include Station Eleven, which has been translated into 33 languages and adapted into a limited series on HBO Max, and The Glass Hotel, which was translated into twenty languages and selected by Barack Obama as one of his favorite books of 2020.

Her most recent novel, Sea of Tranquility, was published in April 2022 and debuted at number three on The New York Times Best Seller list.
