Leigh Bardugo is an American fantasy author best known for her young adult Grishaverse novels, which include the Shadow and Bone trilogy, the Six of Crows duology, and the King of Scars duology. She also received acclaim for her paranormal fantasy adult debut, Ninth House.

Her Shadow and Bone and Six of Crows series have been adapted into a Netflix series titled Shadow and Bone, and Ninth House is set to be adapted by Amazon Studios. Bardugo serves as an executive producer on both adaptations.
