Timothy Francis LaHaye was an American Baptist evangelical Christian minister and political activist. Over the course of his career, he authored more than 85 books, spanning both non-fiction and fiction.

He is perhaps best known for the Left Behind series, a collection of novels depicting apocalypse events following a pre-tribulation rapture.
