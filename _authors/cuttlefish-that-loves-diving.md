Yuan Ye, who writes under the pen name Cuttlefish That Loves Diving, is a Chinese web novelist and advocate from Leshan, Sichuan province. A graduate of Sichuan University, he holds a degree in computer science.

He is best known for his web novel Lord of Mysteries. This work has been adapted into a Chinese animated series, a project that featured direct input from the author.
