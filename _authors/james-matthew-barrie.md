Sir James Matthew Barrie, 1st Baronet, was a British novelist and playwright born and educated in Scotland. After moving to London, he achieved success with numerous novels and plays. He is best remembered as the creator of Peter Pan, a character inspired by the Llewelyn Davies boys whom he met in London.

Barrie first wrote about a baby boy experiencing magical adventures in Kensington Gardens before creating his 1904 West End fairy play, Peter Pan, or The Boy Who Wouldn't Grow Up. The play follows an ageless boy and a girl named Wendy as they embark on adventures in the fantasy setting of Neverland.
