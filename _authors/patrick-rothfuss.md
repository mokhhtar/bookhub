Patrick James Rothfuss is an American author best known for his highly acclaimed series, The Kingkiller Chronicle. The series began with his 2007 debut novel, The Name of the Wind, which won several awards. 

He continued the series with the sequel, The Wise Man's Fear, which was published in 2011 and topped The New York Times Best Seller list.
