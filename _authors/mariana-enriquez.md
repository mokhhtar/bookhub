Mariana Enriquez is an Argentine journalist, novelist, and short story writer who is recognized as part of the group of writers known as the new Argentine narrative. Her work primarily falls within the horror and Latin American Gothic genres.

Her short stories have been featured in numerous international publications, including Granta, Electric Literature, Asymptote, McSweeney's, the Virginia Quarterly Review, and The New Yorker.
