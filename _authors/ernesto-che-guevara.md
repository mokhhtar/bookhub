Ernesto Che Guevara de la Serna was an Argentine Marxist revolutionary, author, guerrilla leader, diplomat, politician, and military theorist. A major figure of the Cuban Revolution, he is recognized for his contributions to political and military thought.

Today, Guevara’s stylized visage is widely recognized as a countercultural symbol of rebellion and a global insignia in popular culture.
