Timothy Francis LaHaye was an American Baptist evangelical Christian minister and political activist. Throughout his career, he authored more than 85 books, spanning both non-fiction and fiction genres.

His notable works include the Left Behind series, a collection of novels that depict apocalypse events occurring after a pre-tribulation rapture.
