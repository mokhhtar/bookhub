Robert Louis Stevenson was a Scottish novelist, essayist, poet, and travel writer. He is best known for his celebrated novels Treasure Island, Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde, and Kidnapped, as well as his poetry collection A Child's Garden of Verses.
