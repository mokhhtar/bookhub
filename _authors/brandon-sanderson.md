Brandon Winn Sanderson is an American author of high fantasy, science fiction, and young adult books. He is best known for his Mistborn series and The Stormlight Archive, both of which are set in his fictional universe known as the Cosmere. Beyond the Cosmere, he has written several young adult and juvenile series, including The Reckoners, the Skyward series, and the Alcatraz series.

In addition to his original work, Sanderson is known for finishing Robert Jordan's high fantasy series, The Wheel of Time. He has also created two graphic novels, White Sand and Dark One.
