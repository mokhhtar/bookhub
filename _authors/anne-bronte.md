Anne Brontë was an English novelist and poet and a member of the renowned Brontë literary family. She was the younger sister of Charlotte, Emily, and Branwell Brontë.

She is best known for her 1847 novel Agnes Grey and her 1848 novel The Tenant of Wildfell Hall. The latter work is considered to be one of the first feminist novels.
