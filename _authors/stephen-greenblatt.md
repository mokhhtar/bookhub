Stephen Jay Greenblatt is an American literary historian and author. Since 2000, he has served as the John Cogan University Professor of the Humanities at Harvard University.

In addition to his academic work, Greenblatt is the general editor of The Norton Shakespeare (2015). He also serves as a general editor and contributor to The Norton Anthology of English Literature.
