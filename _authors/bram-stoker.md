Abraham Stoker was an Irish writer, barrister, literary critic, and theatre manager. He is best known as the author of the 1897 Gothic horror novel Dracula and the creator of its titular character.

The novel and its antagonist are widely regarded as milestones in the fields of Gothic and vampire literature.
