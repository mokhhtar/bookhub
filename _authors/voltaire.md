Voltaire, the pen name of François-Marie Arouet, was a French Enlightenment writer, philosopher, satirist, and historian. He is widely recognized for his wit and his outspoken criticism of both Christianity and slavery.

Throughout his career, Voltaire served as a dedicated advocate for freedom of speech, freedom of religion, and the separation of church and state.
