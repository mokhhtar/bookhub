Harriet A. Washington is an American writer and medical ethicist. She is the author of Medical Apartheid, which received the 2007 National Book Critics Circle Award for Nonfiction.

In addition to her award-winning work, Washington has authored books focusing on the topics of environmental racism and the erosion of informed consent in medicine.
