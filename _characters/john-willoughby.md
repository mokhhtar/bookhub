John Willoughby is a character in Jane Austen's 1811 novel Sense and Sensibility. He is a handsome young man who possesses a small estate and holds expectations of inheriting a large estate from his aunt.

Within the story, Willoughby is in love with Marianne Dashwood, another central character in the novel.
