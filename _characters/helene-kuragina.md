Princess Yelena Vasilyevna Kuragina, known as Hélène, is a fictional character featured in Leo Tolstoy's 1869 novel War and Peace. She has been portrayed by various actresses in adaptations of the work, including Anita Ekberg in the 1956 film and Tuppence Middleton in the 2016 BBC miniseries. Additionally, the character was played by Amber Gray in the New York stage premiere of Natasha, Pierre & The Great Comet of 1812.
