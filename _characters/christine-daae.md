Christine Daaé is the female protagonist of Gaston Leroux's 1910 novel The Phantom of the Opera, as well as its various adaptations. She is a central figure in the story, finding herself the object of affection for both Erik, the Phantom of the Opera, and Viscount Raoul de Chagny.
