Dr. Waldman is a professor at Ingolstadt University who specializes in chemistry. He serves as a mentor to Victor Frankenstein.

The character appears in Mary Shelley's 1818 novel, Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus, as well as in its subsequent film adaptations.
