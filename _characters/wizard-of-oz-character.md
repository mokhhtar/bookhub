The Wizard of Oz, whose full name is Oscar Zoroaster Phadrig Isaac Norman Henkle Emmannuel Ambroise Diggs, is a fictional character residing in the Land of Oz. Created by American author L. Frank Baum, he first appeared in the 1900 book The Wonderful Wizard of Oz.

The character gained further popularity through various adaptations, including a stage play and several films. Among these cinematic appearances is the well-known 1939 MGM musical film.
