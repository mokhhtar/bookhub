The Red Queen is a fictional character who serves as the main antagonist in Lewis Carroll's 1871 fantasy novel Through the Looking-Glass. 

She is frequently confused with the Queen of Hearts from the earlier book Alice's Adventures in Wonderland, though the two characters are very different from one another.
