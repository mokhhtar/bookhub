Tiny Tim Cratchit is a fictional character from the 1843 novella A Christmas Carol by Charles Dickens. Although he appears only briefly in the story, he is considered a major character.

Within the narrative, Tiny Tim serves as an important symbol of the consequences of the protagonist's choices.
