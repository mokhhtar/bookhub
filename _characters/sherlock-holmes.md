Sherlock Holmes is a fictional detective created by British author Arthur Conan Doyle. Within the stories, he refers to himself as a consulting detective and is renowned for his exceptional proficiency in observation, deduction, forensic science, and logical reasoning that borders on the fantastic.

Holmes employs these specialized skills while investigating a wide variety of cases for a diverse range of clients, including Scotland Yard.
