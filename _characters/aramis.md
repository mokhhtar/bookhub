René d'Herblay, known by the alias Aramis, is a fictional character featured in Alexandre Dumas's novels The Three Musketeers, Twenty Years After, and The Vicomte de Bragelonne. 

He serves as one of the three musketeers alongside Athos and Porthos, and all three men are friends of the series' protagonist, d'Artagnan.
