The Sheep is a fictional character created by Charles Lutwidge Dodgson, who wrote under the pen name Lewis Carroll. The character appears in Dodgson's 1871 book, Through the Looking-Glass, which serves as the sequel to his 1865 work, Alice's Adventures in Wonderland.
