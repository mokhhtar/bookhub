Detective Inspector G. Lestrade is a fictional character featured in the Sherlock Holmes stories authored by Arthur Conan Doyle. His literary debut occurred in the 1887 novel A Study in Scarlet.

Lestrade’s final appearance in the series takes place in the 1924 short story The Adventure of the Three Garridebs, which is included in the collection titled The Case-Book of Sherlock Holmes.
