Huckleberry Finn, also known as Huck, is a fictional character created by author Mark Twain. He first appeared in the 1876 novel The Adventures of Tom Sawyer, where he is depicted as being 12 to 13 years old.

Huck later serves as the protagonist and narrator of the sequel, Adventures of Huckleberry Finn, published in 1884, at which time he is a year older. Additionally, he acts as the narrator for two shorter sequels, Tom Sawyer Abroad and Tom Sawyer, Detective, published in 1894 and 1896 respectively.
