Carlotta is a fictional character featured in Gaston Leroux's 1910 novel The Phantom of the Opera. As the leading soprano at the Paris Opera House, she holds a prominent position within the story.

However, her performances are not without controversy. Both the narrator and the Phantom offer criticism of her work, specifically citing a perceived lack of emotion in her singing.
