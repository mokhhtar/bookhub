Captain Nemo is a fictional character created by the French novelist Jules Verne. He is a central figure in Verne's science-fiction literature, appearing in the novels Twenty Thousand Leagues Under the Seas, published in 1870, and The Mysterious Island, published in 1875.

In addition to his roles in these novels, the character makes a brief appearance in the 1882 play Journey Through the Impossible, which was written by Verne in collaboration with Adolphe d'Ennery.
