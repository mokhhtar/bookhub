Athos, also known as the Count de la Fère, is a fictional character featured in Alexandre Dumas's novels The Three Musketeers, Twenty Years After, and The Vicomte de Bragelonne. 

He serves as a highly fictionalised literary representation of the historical musketeer Armand d'Athos, who lived from 1615 to 1643.
