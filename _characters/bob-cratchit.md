Robert Cratchit is a fictional character from the 1843 Charles Dickens novel A Christmas Carol. He serves as the overworked and underpaid clerk for Ebenezer Scrooge.

As a character, Cratchit has come to symbolize the poor working conditions experienced by many working-class people during the early Victorian era, specifically highlighting the struggles of low pay and long working hours.
