A young man with pale skin, green eyes, and extremely red hair who grows to be one of the most notorious magicians and a legendary figure. Raised as part of an Edema Ruh troupe, he survives as a near-feral orphan in Tarbean before entering the University, where he becomes a talented student and skilled at naming and sympathy.
