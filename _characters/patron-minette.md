Patron-Minette is a street gang featured in Victor Hugo's 1862 novel Les Miserables and its musical adaptation. The group is composed of four criminals: Montparnasse, Claquesous, Babet, and Gueulemer or Brujon.

The gang maintains a close acquaintance with the Thenardiers. This connection leads them to be recruited by the couple to assist in the robbery of Jean Valjean.
