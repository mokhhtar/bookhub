Edward Hyde is a murderous criminal featured in the 1886 Gothic horror novella Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde by Scottish author Robert Louis Stevenson. He is involved in a series of strange occurrences with Dr Henry Jekyll, an old friend of the London-based legal practitioner Gabriel John Utterson. Utterson is the character who investigates these mysterious events involving Hyde and Jekyll.
