Madame Thénardier is a fictional character who serves as a secondary antagonist in Victor Hugo's 1862 novel Les Misérables. She is one half of the pair known as the Thénardiers, alongside Monsieur Thénardier.

In addition to her role in the original novel, the character appears in many adaptations of the story across various forms of media.
