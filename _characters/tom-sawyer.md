Thomas Tom Sawyer is the title character of Mark Twain's 1876 novel The Adventures of Tom Sawyer. He is approximately 12 to 13 years old.

In addition to his debut novel, Sawyer appears in three other works by Twain, including Adventures of Huckleberry Finn, Tom Sawyer Abroad, and Tom Sawyer, Detective.
