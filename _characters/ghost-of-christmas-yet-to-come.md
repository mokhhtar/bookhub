The Ghost of Christmas Yet to Come is a fictional character featured in Charles Dickens's 1843 novella A Christmas Carol. As the last of three spirits to visit the miser Ebenezer Scrooge, the figure appears to offer him a final opportunity for redemption. This visitation was originally foretold by the ghost of Scrooge's deceased business partner, Jacob Marley.
