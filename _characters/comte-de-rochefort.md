The Comte de Rochefort is a secondary fictional character featured in the d'Artagnan Romances by Alexandre Dumas. Within the narrative, he is described as being between 40 and 45 years old in the year 1625.

Physically, Rochefort is characterized as a fair-complexioned man who bears a distinct scar across his cheek.
