Nathaniel Natty Bumppo is a fictional character who serves as the protagonist of James Fenimore Cooper's pentalogy of novels, the Leatherstocking Tales. Throughout the series, he is depicted as an archetypal American ranger.

The character has been portrayed numerous times across a variety of media in popular culture.
