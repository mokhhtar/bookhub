Monsieur Thénardier is a fictional character featured in Victor Hugo's 1862 novel Les Misérables. Alongside his wife, Madame Thénardier, he serves as a secondary antagonist in the story.

The character appears in the original novel and has been included in many adaptations of the work across various forms of media.
