Arthur Dimmesdale is a character from Nathaniel Hawthorne’s 1850 historical novel, The Scarlet Letter. Set in the Puritan Massachusetts Bay Colony between 1642 and 1649, the narrative identifies him as the man who conceives a daughter with Hester Prynne, a woman to whom he is not married.

His involvement in this relationship serves as a central element of the story, which explores complex themes of sin, guilt, and legalism. While Hester Prynne faces public punishment for their shared transgression, the novel examines the broader consequences of their actions within the strict religious and historical context of the colony.
