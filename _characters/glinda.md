Glinda, also known as Glinda the Good, is a fictional character created by L. Frank Baum for his Oz novels. She first appeared in his 1900 children's classic, The Wonderful Wizard of Oz.

As the most powerful sorceress in the Land of Oz, Glinda serves as the ruler of the Quadling Country located south of the Emerald City. In addition to her role as a ruler, she acts as the protector of Princess Ozma.
