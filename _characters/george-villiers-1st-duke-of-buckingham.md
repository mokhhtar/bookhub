George Villiers, 1st Duke of Buckingham (1592–1628), was a prominent historical figure who served as a courtier to King James I of England and as a minister to King Charles I. 

He is the father of George Villiers, the 2nd Duke of Buckingham, who later became an English politician and lived in exile with King Charles II.
