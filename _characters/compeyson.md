Compeyson serves as the primary antagonist in Charles Dickens' 1861 novel Great Expectations. Described as a man reminiscent of the character George Wickham, he is a criminal whose actions caused harm to two individuals. These two people subsequently played a significant role in shaping the life of the protagonist, Pip.
