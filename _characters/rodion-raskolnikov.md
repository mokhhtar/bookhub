Rodion Romanovich Raskolnikov is the fictional protagonist of the 1866 novel Crime and Punishment by Fyodor Dostoyevsky. His surname is derived from the Russian word raskolnik, which means schismatic, while his first name, Rodion, originates from Greek and signifies an inhabitant of Rhodes.
