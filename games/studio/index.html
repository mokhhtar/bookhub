The day

The bot answers nothing. It shows the pair, waits long enough for whoever is watching to make up their own mind, and then turns the card over. A bot picking correctly five times is a video about a bot; a bot picking wrong is an advertisement for the machine having written the better passage. Neither is the video. The viewer plays this one.

Which day

Pacing

Leave the reading beat blank and it is worked out from how long the two passages actually are — 180 characters and 420 characters are not the same wait — clamped to 8–20 seconds. This is the number that decides whether the video works: too short and the viewer has not finished the second passage when the answer lands, so they never played and the reveal means nothing.

Five pairs runs about three minutes. Two is the shape of a feed clip, and it ends on a reveal, which is a better place to stop than a score. Showing all five is the only setting that plays the closing card.

Demo mode writes no localStorage and reports no score to the games Worker, so a morning of takes cannot turn up in the “how many people solved this” the video is advertising.

Recording

How

This is the size of the page, and for the file mode it is also the size of the video — the recorder captures the viewport in CSS pixels and no scale factor raises that. Widening it does not enlarge the game either: the stage is capped at 60ch, so past about 700 you get the whole desktop page, nav bar and footer and all, with the card as a small box in the top third. Record narrow and scale up below. Above 560 the page uses its wider layout; below 480 it changes again.

On the way out

The bitrate is a ceiling, not a quota — a page that sits still between taps does not need it, and a 116 second take asking for 8M came out at 1.9. “Fit inside the safe zone” shrinks the whole picture until nothing in it can land under TikTok’s buttons; it is smaller, and the check below is how you decide whether you need it.

Both bots refuse to record against a build older than the page source — a take of a stale build is a take of the wrong game. That check is on the file timestamp, and git rewrites timestamps on every pull whether or not a byte changed, which here is routine. So “the built page is older than…” often means nothing was edited at all. Rebuild and run again; it takes about thirty seconds and is usually a no-op.

Safe zone

Drawn on frames taken back out of the finished file, so it shows where the safe zone falls after the scaling and padding — not where it would fall in the browser, which is a different rectangle. Red is what TikTok covers with its own interface: the header at the top, the caption and sound bar at the bottom, the like/comment/share rail on the right. Anything inside the green outline is yours.

These margins are a starting point and nothing more. Published figures for the same year disagree by as much as 150px on the bottom bar alone, and TikTok moves its interface several times a year. The only authority is a real upload looked at on a real phone — check one before trusting this on anything that matters.
